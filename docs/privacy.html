Re:T-UI Files Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 August 2026

Re:T-UI Files is a local Android file manager by DvilSpawn. It does not include advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud storage, or automatic crash reporting.

Files and storage

The app requests All files access because browsing and managing files across shared storage is its core purpose. File names, paths, contents, favorites, recent folders, theme preferences, and trash metadata are processed locally on your device. The developer does not receive this information.

APK files

When you explicitly open a local APK, the app can hand it to Android so you can choose an available installer. Re:T-UI Files does not silently install apps, install apps in the background, or use this capability to update itself outside Google Play.

Sharing and other apps

When you choose Open or Share, Android may send the selected file to another app you choose. That app's privacy policy then applies. Re:T-UI Files does not upload or transmit files to the developer.

Deletion and retention

Favorites, recent folders, preferences, and trash metadata remain on the device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Files managed outside the app's private storage remain under your control. Trashed files remain locally until restored, permanently deleted, or removed by the app's configured cleanup behavior.

Children

Re:T-UI Files is a productivity tool and is not directed to children.

Contact

For privacy questions, email DvilSpawn@gmail.com or open an issue in the project repository.
