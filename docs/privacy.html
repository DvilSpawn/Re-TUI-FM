Re:T-UI Files Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026. Re:T-UI Files is developed by DvilSpawn.

Re:T-UI Files processes your files locally on your Android device. The developer does not collect or transmit your personal data. The app has no advertising, analytics, accounts, or automatic crash reporting.

File access and storage

All files access enables the app's core file-manager functions: browsing, searching, editing, copying, moving, archiving, sharing, and deleting files. File paths, favorites, recent folders, preferences, and trash metadata are stored locally. App backup and device transfer are disabled.

Sharing and APK opening

When you choose to share or open a file, Android grants the selected app access to that file. That app's privacy policy applies to its handling of the file. Opening a local APK hands it to an installer you choose; Re:T-UI Files does not install apps silently or download updates.

Your controls

You can revoke file access or clear app data in Android settings. Files and trash stored in shared storage remain until you delete them; uninstalling the app does not remove them automatically.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the project issue tracker. Do not post private files or personal information in public issues. Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
